# Cases (dossiers)

Design for suveryn-tracker#10, decided with Filip on 16 October 2026. A **case** is the file a
confidential profession works on: a notary's dossier, a lawyer's matter, a patent application, a
patient's file, a government case. Today every document and conversation belongs to the person who
uploaded or started it; with cases, colleagues work on the same file.

What sūveryn is here: the confidential AI workspace for a case. What it is not: the office's system
of record. The practice-management software keeps the file, its deadlines and its legal archive;
sūveryn keeps what the AI needs, for as long as the office says, and no longer.

## Decisions

| # | Decision |
|---|---|
| 1 | The case **reference** is the office's own file number, typed in as the office writes it. Sūveryn keeps its own UUID underneath. |
| 2 | **No client names** in the reference or the case name. The parties are information inside the case, read from its documents (the facts layer, #27), never a label on the outside. |
| 3 | The **default access** for a new case is a setting per appliance: *office-wide* (everyone signed in sees it; notary offices, where all staff share the same secrecy) or *members only* (law firms with conflict walls, healthcare, government units). The web setup asks none of the settings: an appliance starts with the fail-safe values (members only, closed cases kept until deleted by hand), and an administrator sets them in Administration › Settings. |
| 4 | A manager can **restrict** any case to its members, whatever the default. |
| 5 | **Every user may create** a case. |
| 6 | A case always has exactly one **manager**. When a manager's account is to be removed, an administrator hands their cases to someone else first; the account can't go while it manages a case. |
| 7 | Every document records its **origin**: uploaded, generated by sūveryn (a Word export, a draft), or produced by a playbook. **Only uploaded documents are sources** for answers: the AI never cites its own earlier output as evidence. |
| 8 | A document belongs to **one case**. The same file needed in two cases is uploaded twice. De-duplication is per case. |
| 9 | **No cadence of its own.** Sūveryn tracks open or closed with its dates, the case type, and playbook progress (#28). No stages, deadlines or signing dates: those live in the practice software and would disagree. |
| 10 | A **closed** case is read-only: no uploads, no new questions, no playbook runs. It can be **reopened** while it is kept. |
| 11 | The office **chooses how long closed cases are kept**: 6 months, 1 year, 5 years, or until deleted by hand. Nothing is chosen for them. A month before a case is due to be deleted, its manager and the administrators see it listed and can extend it, reopen it, or mark it *keep*. Then sūveryn deletes the case with its documents, index, facts, runs and conversations (#31 says what deleting guarantees). |
| 12 | **Conversations** in a case are private to whoever started them, with a button to share one with the case's members. A shared conversation is read-only for the others. |
| 13 | A **personal space** stays for quick questions outside any case. Today's documents and conversations move there, per owner. |
| 14 | (= 3) The default access is per appliance. |
| 15 | **Every access to a case is logged**: who, when, what (opened, read a document, asked, exported, changed members, closed). Viewable by the case's manager and the administrators; kept as long as the sign-in events (180 days, #16), then deleted. |

## Roles

| Role | May |
|---|---|
| **Reader** (observer) | Open the case, read its documents, see shared conversations, see playbook results, export |
| **Contributor** | Also upload documents, ask questions (own conversations), run playbooks, approve or edit at checkpoints |
| **Manager** | Also add and remove members, change roles, restrict or open the case, close and reopen it, delete documents, mark *keep* |
| **Administrator** (Keycloak role `suveryn-admin`) | On every case: see it exists and who manages it, hand it to another manager, see its access log, set the keep period. Not read its documents, unless a member |

In an office-wide appliance, everyone who isn't a member is a reader of every unrestricted case.
Membership adds rights; it never takes them away.

## Case types and what a case is about

The **type** says what kind of file it is and decides which playbooks fit: for notaries, sale,
company formation, estate, gift, marriage contract; other sectors bring their own list. Types are
data, not code: a short list per appliance, with sūveryn's own per sector.

With the type comes a **subject**: what the case is about, in the type's terms. A property for a
sale, a person for a patient file, an application number for a patent, a dispute for a matter. One
short free text and, where the type defines them, a few named fields the playbooks read.

## The data model

```
cases             id, reference, name, type, subject jsonb, access (default|restricted),
                  state (open|closed), opened_at, closed_at, keep_until, keep (bool), confidential (bool),
                  manager (Keycloak sub), created_by, created_at, updated_at
case_members      case_id, user (sub), role (reader|contributor|manager), added_by, added_at, username, name
documents         + case_id (null = personal space), origin (uploaded|generated|playbook|answer), owner
conversations     + case_id (null = personal), shared (bool)          (owner stays: who started it)
case_events       id, case_id, user, at, action, detail jsonb          (the access log, 180 days)
case_pins         case_id, user, pinned_at                             (each person's own pins)
case_settings     key, value, updated_at, updated_by                   (Administration › Settings)
departed_accounts sub, username, name, deleted_at, deleted_by          (deleted accounts' names)
playbook_runs     + case_id (#27, #28)
```

- A document's column stays `owner`: the uploader. The API calls it `uploaded_by`. The case, not the
  uploader, decides who may read it.
- Personal space is `case_id IS NULL`, authorised by `uploaded_by`/`owner` as today.
- Deleting a case deletes everything with its `case_id` (cascade), including chunks and facts, then
  the files in the work directory. Deleting a document inside a case: as today, plus its facts out
  of results and earlier runs marked out of date (#28).

## Authorisation

One function decides every request: `may(user, case, action)`.

1. An administrator may `list`, `handover`, `read_log`, `set_keep`; nothing else without membership.
2. A member's role gives the rights in the table above.
3. In an office-wide appliance, a non-member is a reader of an unrestricted case.
4. A closed case allows reading and exporting only, for everyone; and `reopen` for its manager.
5. Personal space: the owner only, as today.

Every check runs on the server (api-gateway); the UI only hides what it already knows is refused.
Documents stored before cases existed have no `case_id` and keep their owner: fail closed, as today.

## The screens (chat-ui)

- **Cases list**: open cases first, by last activity; reference, name, type, manager, my role; a
  search on reference and name; closed cases behind a filter.
- **A case**: its documents (with origin, uploader and date), its conversations (mine, and shared),
  playbook results, members. "Ask about this case" starts a conversation limited to it.
- **New case**: reference, name, type, subject; the creator is its manager; access from the
  appliance's default, changeable here.
- **Closing**: a confirmation that says what closing means and when the case will be deleted under
  the office's keep period.
- **Administration**: *Cases* lists every case with its manager, the cases due for deletion and
  handover; *Settings* holds the default access and the keep periods.

## Sequencing

1. **Cases with a single member.** The entity, the data model, personal space, the migration of
   today's documents, "ask about this case", and the authorisation function with its tests. Enough
   for the playbook runner (#28) to build on a `case_id`.
2. **Members and roles**, the office-wide default, restriction, the access log.
3. **Closing, keep period and deletion**, with #31.
4. **Shared conversations** and the admin dashboard's case pages.

Out of scope here: several people in one live conversation (a separate concept, as #10 says);
syncing cases with the practice software (a connector, later).

## Built

- **Stage 1** (core PR #43): the case, the personal space, "ask about this case", `may`.
- **Stage 2** (core PR #44, appliance #35): members and roles, the office's directory, handover, the access log, `SUVERYN_CASES_DEFAULT_ACCESS`.
- **Stage 3**: closing, the keep period and deletion. `POST /v1/cases/{id}/close`, `/reopen`, `/keep` and `GET /v1/cases/due`; the office's period is `SUVERYN_CASES_KEEP` (`6m`, `1y`, `5y`, `manual`), from the appliance's `--cases-keep` or `CASES_KEEP`, default `manual` since nothing is chosen for the office. Closing sets `keep_until` one period ahead; from 30 days before, the case is listed for its manager and the administrators, who can extend it by one period, reopen it, or mark it *keep*; an hourly sweep in the gateway deletes what is due, the way deleting a case does (a case left because a store wasn't reachable yet, as during a restart, is looked at again within two minutes). Decided while building: `set_keep` (extend, keep) is a right on a *closed* case, for its manager and the administrators; the office's period itself is the appliance setting (changed in Administration › Settings since stage 4). The confirmation and the warning are worded in the three languages in the chat UI (`closeCase*`, `deletedOn*` in `i18n/`); what deletion guarantees on the appliance is #31's.
- **Stage 4**: shared conversations and the administrator's case pages. `POST /v1/conversations/{id}/share` (its owner, in an open case) makes a conversation readable for everyone who may read the case, read-only for them; the list in a case shows one's own and the shared ones (`owner`, `shared`), and the access log notes shares. Deleting a case's document now cleans every member's conversations, not only the deleter's. The administrator's pages: `GET /v1/admin/cases` (every case with its manager and member count, nothing from inside), hand over from there, and `GET`/`PUT /v1/admin/cases/settings` for the default access and the keep period, stored in `case_settings` on top of what the appliance was provisioned with (`SUVERYN_CASES_DEFAULT_ACCESS`, `SUVERYN_CASES_KEEP` stay the initial values). Decided while building: a shared conversation can't be continued by others (decision 12 says read-only; several people in one live conversation stays out of scope); sharing needs the contributor's right and an open case; a changed keep period applies to cases closed from then on (a closed case keeps its date). The web setup asks none of this (it throws as few hurdles as possible: the appliance is provisioned with the fail-safe defaults, members only and kept until deleted by hand), and Administration › Settings is where the office sets them. The same page carries the third office setting, how long saved conversations are kept (`conversations_keep`, `SUVERYN_CONVERSATIONS_KEEP` as provisioned; the data lifecycle, suveryn-tracker#31), with the same periods. **Administration › Settings** (admin.md, 28 October): opening the default access to the office applies to new cases or, by choice, to all cases. "New cases" restricts the existing cases that followed the default, so they stay with their members (a manager can open one); "All cases" opens every case, those a manager restricted too, with a line in each access log. Closing it to the members applies to every case that follows the default. Strictly confidential cases never change. The page counts first (`/v1/admin/cases/settings/preview`) and asks for confirmation with the number.
